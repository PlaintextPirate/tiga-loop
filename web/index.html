Tiga Loop

Trim, crop and send GIFs to the screen on your Zoom75 TIGA. Everything happens on-device.

This page needs Chrome or Edge on a computer: it talks to the keyboard over Web Bluetooth, which Safari and Firefox don't support.

Preview uses the screen's 16-bit colour. Playback speed is approximate.

  1. Choose a GIF or picture

  2. Trim

    Keep only the part you want; long GIFs look smoother when trimmed than when thinned.

  3. Frame it

    Drag the box to choose what shows on the screen. Scroll or use the slider to zoom.

  4. Frames and speed

    Up to 50 frames. More frames means smoother motion and a longer upload (about 8 seconds per frame).

  5. Send to the keyboard

    Keep the keyboard plugged into USB power while it uploads, and avoid typing on it. On battery it falls asleep mid-upload.

    If an upload fails

    Before the tiger animation appears, nothing changed on the keyboard. Just send again.

    After it appears, wait two minutes and send once more. If the screen stays stuck, it needs a full power-off: unplugging USB isn't enough because it runs on its battery. Pull off the case, disconnect the battery from the board for at least 30 seconds, press a few keys, then reconnect.

About

Your upload replaces the screen's one custom slot. CPU and GPU temperatures for the stats page need the small tiga-stats command-line tool, because browsers can't read sensors.
